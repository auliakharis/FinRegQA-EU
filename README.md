Pairwise LLM-as-a-Judge in the Financial Domain

Large language models (LLMs) has been used massively on financial services industry, from sentiment analysis,
risk assessment to generating investment insights and personalized financial advice. At the same time, Rapid
advances in LLMs can bring a range of challenges, such as issues related to robustness, security, fairness, and
adherence to regulatory requirements. Thus, the concept of using an LLM as a judge emerged, in order to verify
whether the outputs of LLMs remain within the specified scope.

We will design two sets of evaluation queries: (i) assistant-facing finance-domain prompts, and (ii) LLM-as-a-
judge prompts with rubrics for scoring the assistants’ outputs (including pairwise comparisons)
